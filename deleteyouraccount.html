Delete Your Account

Solve to Wake – Loud Alarm

Your data, your choice. You can delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time, either from within the App or by submitting a request on this page.

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently deleted from our servers:

  • Firebase account: Your user ID, name, email address, and authentication record.
  • Cloud alarm data: All alarm configurations synced to Firebase Firestore (time, days, labels, challenge settings, and all other alarm parameters).
  • Cloud settings: All app preferences synced to Firestore (vibration, snooze, volume, fade-in, dark mode, and other settings).

⚠ This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Once deleted, your cloud data cannot be recovered. Make sure you no longer need your synced alarms before proceeding.

What Is NOT Affected

  • Local data on your device (alarms, settings, barcode images) is not deleted by account deletion. To remove local data, clear the App's data from your device settings or uninstall the App.
  • Anonymized analytics data collected via Google Analytics for Firebase is not linked to your account and cannot be individually deleted.

How to Delete Your Account

Option 1: From Within the App (Recommended)

  • Open Solve to Wake – Loud Alarm on your device.
  • Go to Settings (gear icon).
  • Tap your profile / account section.
  • Tap "Delete Account".
  • Confirm the deletion when prompted. Your account and all cloud data will be deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request Deletion via This Page

If you no longer have access to the App or cannot sign in, you can request account deletion by submitting the form below. We will process your request within 3 business days.

Data Retention Timeline

  • In-app deletion: Immediate. All cloud data is deleted as soon as you confirm.
  • Web form request: Processed within 3 business days.
  • Backup systems: Residual data in automated backups (if any) is purged within 30 days.
